# 10x — 2,700 → 27,000 clicks/month by end of January 2027

Written 30 July 2026, from the 28-day GSC export. This is the ambitious-case
plan the owner asked for: not "grow nicely", but the specific world in which
traffic 10x-es in 6 months, decomposed into levers with numbers, and what each
lever demands.

## The math that frames everything

Current: **2,698 clicks / 28 days, growing +22% per period.** That growth rate,
merely *sustained* for 6 periods, compounds to **~8,900/month — a 3.3x on
momentum alone.** So:

> **10x = keeping the +22% flywheel spinning × a 3x from new levers on top.**

The corollary: protecting the current growth engines (EN commercial cluster,
ES/FR content, brand) is half the plan. The other half must come from things
that don't exist yet — because incremental SEO on the current surface cannot
produce another 18,000 clicks.

And one number defines the ceiling today:

> **Basketball: 0 impressions. Esports: 0. Every sport that is not football: 0.**
> Football is 100% of the query universe. The biggest multiplier available is
> not ranking better — it is playing on more boards.

## The flywheel (the owner's thesis, made explicit)

The product-led loop the whole plan feeds:

```
better 3D tool → more designs made → more kits produced → more teams wearing
them → more team pages, share pages, photos, brand searches → more authority
→ better rankings → more designers arrive → better 3D tool (revenue funds it)
```

Every lever below either widens a stage of this loop or removes friction from
it. The tool is the engine; SEO is the transmission. "Good products market
themselves" is true **only if the product's output is visible and indexable** —
that is the plan's job.

## The six levers, ranked by expected clicks

### 1. EN commercial cluster → top 3 (+5,000/mo) — the authority war

357 queries (`jersey maker`, `custom football jersey`, `football shirt
maker`…): 29,057 impressions/28d, weighted position **11.2**, 2.7% CTR,
791 clicks. This is the single biggest existing asset and it sits at the
bottom of page 1 / top of page 2.

Top-3 CTR on commercial queries is 10–15%, and impressions grow as rank
improves (coverage widens). At position ≤4 on ~40k impressions: **~5,000
clicks/mo from this cluster alone.**

What it demands — and be honest about this, it is the binding constraint of
every "top 3" ambition in this document: **domain authority. Links.** On-page
is largely done; position 11 → 4 against established competitors is won
off-site. The link engine, in order of leverage:

- **The concept-to-real story is genuinely press-able.** Fan concept kits go
  viral on their own; "the factory that makes viral AI concepts real" is a
  story for football media, design media, and AI media simultaneously. Pitch
  it. Every team story ("Sunday league team wears ChatGPT kit") is local-press
  bait too.
- **Original data studies, quarterly.** "What 1,000 fan concepts taught us
  about kit design" / "The colours amateur teams actually choose" — original
  numbers are what journalists and LLMs cite. We have the data; nobody else
  does.
- **The free 3D tool as a linkable asset.** Design-tool roundups, teacher/club
  resource lists, subreddit/Discord kit-design communities. The tool is free —
  that is the whole pitch.

Target: **cluster weighted position ≤5 by November, ≤4 by January. 5,000
clicks/mo.**

### 2. Basketball — the tool already has it, the internet doesn't know (+3,000/mo)

universe rivals the football cluster in volume and is strongest in the US —
so basketball launches **US-first, fused with lever 3**: the US property
carries football + basketball from day one, doubling the query surface of the
same build.

The sequence, now all surface work:

1. RTP basketball drop (4–6 designs on the existing model) with keyword-built
   pages (see lever 5's model-page pattern), EN first.
2. Basketball block on the www homepage + nav; hub page
   (`custom-basketball-jerseys`) + 3–4 cluster posts in the US lexicon.
3. Tool entry page `/3d/basketball` targeting `basketball jersey maker` /
   `basketball uniform designer` — the tool is free and real; nobody else
   offers a live 3D basketball designer at this price point.
4. ES second (basketball culture), then the other locales with the season.
5. Sport #3 (futsal is half-owned; esports is production-identical) triggers
   when basketball shows impressions — expect faster than football took,
   since the domain now has authority football didn't start with.

Target: **basketball surfaced everywhere by September, 1,500–3,000 clicks/mo
from non-football clusters by January.**

### 5. The 3D tool as an SEO surface (+2,000/mo) — product-led pages

`design.momuto.com` has **one indexable URL**. The tool produces thousands of
designs; none of them are visible to search. That is the flywheel's biggest
leak, and the fix is the most defensible content on the internet because
competitors cannot fake it:

- **Public design/share pages**: every shared design gets a rich page (render,
  colourway, sport, "make it yours" CTA). The 115 team pages already prove the
  pattern ranks. At tool volume this is thousands of long-tail pages that are
  *product output*, not content marketing. Guard rails: rich pages only
  (render + structured specs + CTA), noindex anything thin, sitemap per
  section.
- **Tool entry pages per sport × per design** (`/3d/basketball`,
  `/3d/the-apex`…): the "free 3D designer" query family (846 impressions at
  position 11.5 today, CTR 3.5%) is honestly ours — the tool IS free. Own the
  entire `free` cluster with pages that route into the tool.
- **RTP model expansion as keyword surface — 7 → 25 models.** Every model is
  a page per locale with title, description and deep link into the tool, and
  today's 7 were never designed to rank. Each new model gets a *search-shaped
  identity*: named and described against a real design-style query niche
  (striped / gradient / retro / camo / two-tone / sash / hooped…), so the
  catalogue itself becomes a keyword estate across all five sites. The 3D
  pipeline makes each model cheap; the naming/metadata is where the SEO lives.
  Basketball models multiply this again.
- **Country & national concept series.** Competitors rank with these, and our
  own data already proves the demand: `maillot concept france` (370 impr),
  `maillot france concept`, `…bresil`, `…japon`, `maillot psg concept` all
  appear in GSC, the FR concepts collection sits at 2,201 impressions
  (position 7.3), and the Asperion France concept product page pulls 349
  impressions on its own. The Blue Samurai Japan collab and the
  regions-of-Spain tributes are the same pattern already working. Build it as
  a deliberate series: one unofficial concept design per major football
  nation (France, Italy, Brazil, Japan, Argentina, England…), each an RTP-style
  page + customisable in 3D, no federation crests (the Real-Madrid rule
  applies), collection hubs per locale. Tournament cycles (Euro 2028, WC 2030
  qualifiers) spike these queries predictably — the series is evergreen
  inventory that peaks on schedule.
- **Gallery → tool loop**: every RTP model page and gallery entry deep-links
  into the tool pre-loaded; every tool session ends with a shareable URL.

Target: **design.momuto.com from ~1 ranking URL to 500+ indexed rich pages;
2,000 clicks/mo on the tool + free clusters combined.**

## What breaks at 10x — fix before it does

Traffic is the input; these are the bottlenecks that turn 10x traffic into
angry customers instead of 10x revenue:

1. **Design studio throughput.** 10x deposit briefs at 24–48h mockup promise.
   The AI-assisted design pipeline (concept → production file) needs to get
   faster per order, or the promise breaks exactly when it matters most.
2. **Checkout paths.** IT still cannot convert RTP→3D→cart
   (`checkSumbit`/`GoodInfoAction` — the standing blocker). Every locale must
   have a tested end-to-end order path *before* its traffic arrives.
3. **Support in five languages** at 10x volume — templates + the FAQ/LLM layer
   absorb the repetitive load, but the WhatsApp promise needs staffing.
4. **Production lead time honesty.** 25–30 days survives 10x only if the
   factory relationship scales; the moment it slips, the comparison pages'
   strongest claim dies. Monitor and update copy *before* reality diverges.

## Cadence
