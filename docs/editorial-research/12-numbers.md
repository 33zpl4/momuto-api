# The Numbers: A Citable Fact Bank for Grassroots Football and Kit Culture

**Research area 12 — quantitative backbone for MOMUTO editorial.** Compiled 6 August 2026.

This dossier collects verifiable figures on grassroots football participation (England, Spain, France, Italy, Germany, US), volunteer numbers in amateur football, replica kit sales records, custom teamwear industry lead times, seasonality of team kit ordering, and football merchandise market sizes. Every figure carries its measurement definition, source, year, and URL. Where sources conflict — which is common, because "players" can mean registered licence-holders, club members, or anyone who kicked a ball once last year — both figures are given with a caveat. Figures marked "reported via" were confirmed only through secondary coverage of a primary source; treat them as one verification step weaker than figures taken directly from a federation or company document.

---

## 1. Participation

### England
- **15.7 million people play football in England (2024)** — total participation across all formats, per The FA's grassroots strategy launch — The FA / England Football, 2024 — https://www.thefa.com/news/2024/oct/28/a-new-landmark-strategy-20241028 and https://www.englandfootball.com/articles/2024/Oct/28/A-new-landmark-strategy-20241028
- **4.2 million people played football at least once a week (2024)** — regular (weekly) participation — reported in coverage of the FA strategy — Team Grassroots, 2024 — https://www.teamgrassroots.co.uk/has-2024-been-a-good-year-for-grassroots-football/
- **~125,000 teams across all levels of English football (2024)** — affiliated team count — The FA grassroots strategy, 2024 — https://www.thefa.com/news/2024/oct/28/a-new-landmark-strategy-20241028
- **+220,000 new players and 15,000 new teams targeted by 2028** — FA growth projection in the 2024–28 grassroots strategy — The FA, 2024 — https://www.thefa.com/news/2024/oct/28/a-new-landmark-strategy-20241028
- **£15.9bn per year — social and economic value of grassroots football in England (2024, up from £10.2bn in 2021)** — FA-commissioned social value measurement — The FA, 2024 — https://www.thefa.com/news/2024/oct/28/a-new-landmark-strategy-20241028

### Germany
- **8,005,050 memberships across 23,868 clubs (2024/25)** — DFB club memberships, first time above 8 million in the federation's 125-year history; +3.86% year on year — DFB Mitgliederstatistik, 2025 — https://www.dfb.de/news/dfb-mitgliederstatistik-mehr-schiris-mehr-qualifizierte-und-rekord-zum-jubilaeum (English coverage: https://sports.yahoo.com/article/dfb-reaches-8-million-members-102029443.html)
- **2.38 million active players (2024/25 season)** — people actually playing in German clubs (memberships ≠ active players); +2.15% year on year, with girls under 16 the fastest-growing group at +9% — DFB, 2025 — https://www.dfb.de/news/dfb-mitgliederstatistik-mehr-schiris-mehr-qualifizierte-und-rekord-zum-jubilaeum
- **~+1 million memberships gained since 2021** — post-pandemic growth of German club football — DFB, 2025 — same URL as above

### France
- **2,384,192 licences (2023–24 season) — all-time FFF record** — total licences including players, coaches, referees, officials; +7.6% year on year — Fédération Française de Football, 2024 — https://www.fff.fr/article/12662-pres-de-2-4-millions-de-licences-en-2023-2024.html
- **2,007,407 practising players (2023–24)**, of whom **202,253 female practitioners (+11.2% vs 2022–23)** — FFF, 2024 — https://www.fff.fr/article/12662-pres-de-2-4-millions-de-licences-en-2023-2024.html

### Italy
- **1,131,906 registered male and female players (2023–24), +2% year on year** — FIGC tesserati (players only) — FIGC ReportCalcio, 15th edition, 2025 (with AREL and PwC Italy) — https://www.figc.it/media/277126/report-calcio-2025.pdf (news summary: https://www.figc.it/it/giovani/news/lattivit%C3%A0-giovanile-in-crescita-del-36-i-dati-del-reportcalcio-sul-calcio-di-base/)
- **~1.5 million total FIGC registrations (2023–24)** — includes technical staff, referees, officials; FIGC alone accounts for ~30% of all registered athletes across Italy's 50 sports federations — FIGC ReportCalcio 2025 — https://www.figc.it/media/277126/report-calcio-2025.pdf
- **~900,000 youth players; 22.7% of all Italian children aged 5–16 are FIGC-registered (2023–24, vs 14.4% in 2020–21)** — FIGC ReportCalcio 2025 — https://www.figc.it/it/giovani/news/lattivit%C3%A0-giovanile-in-crescita-del-36-i-dati-del-reportcalcio-sul-calcio-di-base/
- **45,620 registered female players (2024, up from 18,854 in 2008)** — FIGC ReportCalcio 2025 — same URL

### Spain
- **~1.2–1.25 million football licences (2023)** — football is Spain's largest federated sport at 29.2% of all 4,271,300 federated sport licences (which implies ~1.25m); derived from the CSD's Anuario de Estadísticas Deportivas / Estadística del Deporte Federado 2023 — CSD via ADESP, 2024 — https://adesp.es/el-numero-total-de-licencias-federadas-deportivas-en-2023-crece-hasta-los-4-271-300/ and https://www.csd.gob.es/es/espana-supera-por-primera-vez-en-su-historia-los-4-millones-de-deportistas-federados
- **107,853 women's football licences (2023)** — RFEF female licences, per CSD statistics — via Statista, 2024 — https://es.statista.com/estadisticas/1229474/futbol-numero-de-mujeres-federadas-en-espana-por-comunidad-autonoma
- The RFEF's own licence breakdown for 2023–24 is published at https://rfef.es/sites/default/files/2024-11/LICENCIAS_2324.pdf (Memoria de Actividades 2023–24) — **the exact RFEF grand total could not be extracted during this research** (the PDF blocked automated retrieval); the ~1.25m figure above is the CSD-derived estimate. Flag for manual verification.

### United States
- **20.5 million total soccer participants (2024)** — played soccer at least once in the year (outdoor + indoor + futsal, casual and organized); outdoor soccer +8.1% year on year — SFIA (Sports & Fitness Industry Association) participation data, 2024/25 — https://sfia.org/u-s-soccer-participation-data/ and https://sfia.org/resources/team-sports-category-records-highest-number-of-participation-in-nearly-10-years/
- **16.8 million outdoor soccer participants (2025, +15.8% YoY); 6.6 million indoor (+10.5%)** — SFIA, 2025/26 — https://sgbonline.com/exec-sfia-sees-ripe-conditions-for-soccer-participation-surge-post-world-cup/
- **4+ million registered youth players across US Youth Soccer, US Club Soccer and AYSO, at 10,000+ clubs** — registered (pay-to-play organized) youth football — compiled from federation figures, 2025/26 — https://withcentro.com/blog/us-youth-soccer-participation-statistics-2026
- **~3 million registered players annually in US Youth Soccer alone (decade average)** — US Youth Soccer via same compilation — https://withcentro.com/blog/us-youth-soccer-participation-statistics-2026
- **29 million participants projected within a year of the 2026 World Cup** — US Soccer Federation projection (aspiration, not measurement) — reported by SGB Media, 2026 — https://sgbonline.com/exec-sfia-sees-ripe-conditions-for-soccer-participation-surge-post-world-cup/

**Conflicts and caveats (Participation).** These figures are not comparable across countries: England's 15.7m and the US's 20.5m are "played at all in the past year" survey numbers; France (2.38m licences), Italy (1.13m players), Spain (~1.25m licences) and Germany (2.38m active players) are registration counts — a much stricter definition. Germany's headline 8m is *memberships* (including non-playing club members), not players; always pair it with the 2.38m active-player figure. Spain's total is derived, not primary — verify against the RFEF PDF before print use. US figures move fast year to year (SFIA 2024 vs 2025 differ substantially); date every citation.

## 2. Volunteers in Amateur Football

- **1.4 million people volunteer in football in England** — broad volunteering figure used by County FAs — London FA, 2024 — https://www.londonfa.com/volunteers
- **"Over one million volunteers, including 160,000 coaches and 37,000 referees"** — FA figure for the grassroots game in England — The FA / England Football volunteering pages and parliamentary written evidence, c. 2024 — https://www.englandfootball.com/participate/volunteer and https://committees.parliament.uk/writtenevidence/134345/html/
- **~1.7 million volunteers in German amateur football, often投 investing 15–20 hours per week; theoretical value €2.18bn/year** — DFB via its Ehrenamt (volunteering) analysis and SROI study across ~24,500 amateur clubs — DFB, reported via DFL, 2022 — https://www.dfl.de/en/news/thanks-to-the-volunteers-2022/ and https://www.dfb.de/en/news/detail/amateurs-football-is-there-for-everyone-113804/
- **1.5 million grassroots volunteers thanked in the DFB's #ehrenamtistunbezahlbar campaign** — DFB campaign figure — via UEFA.com, 2021/22 — https://www.uefa.com/news-media/news/0264-10fe1c3844c9-93cbe2aefe4d-1000--dfb-campaign-thanks-1-5-million-grassroots-volunteers/
- **8.6 million registered footballers across 25 UEFA national associations generate €39.5bn in cumulative economic, social and health value** — UEFA GROW Social Return on Investment model — UEFA, c. 2022–23 — https://www.uefa.com/insideuefa/social-responsibility/news/0264-1103f88152c8-a8f3fed3dbc1-1000--celebrating-volunteers-contribution-to-football/
- **€44 million over four years — UEFA's grassroots investment across its 55 member associations** — UEFA HatTrick/grassroots commitment — UEFA — https://www.uefa.com/development/grassroots/overview/

**Conflicts and caveats (Volunteers).** England's "over 1 million" (FA) and "1.4 million" (London FA / wider-football definition) coexist; Germany's 1.5m (campaign) vs 1.7m (Ehrenamt analysis) likewise. These are estimates, not censuses — no federation maintains a live volunteer register. Safest editorial phrasing: "well over a million volunteers in each of England and Germany."

## 3. Replica Kit Sales Records

- **3 million pre-orders for Nigeria's 2018 World Cup home shirt (Nike); sold out within minutes of release (reports of ~3 minutes online), $90 retail, queues outside Nike's London Oxford Street store** — pre-order figure stated by the Nigerian Football Federation — CNBC, 2018 — https://www.cnbc.com/2018/06/22/nikes-nigerian-world-cup-jersey-breaks-sales-records.html ; Quartz Africa, 2018 — https://qz.com/africa/1290171/nigeria-nike-world-cup-soccer-jersey-pre-order-sales-top-three-million
- **£32.5m of Cristiano Ronaldo Manchester United shirts sold in the first 12 hours after his squad number was confirmed (Sept 2021) — a Premier League record; also reported ~300,000 units in 12 hours** — estimate by retail platform LovetheSales.com, NOT an official adidas/Manchester United figure — GiveMeSport, 2021 — https://www.givemesport.com/1748046-cristiano-ronaldo-shirt-sales-fans-spend-325m-in-the-first-12-hours/ ; unit figure via https://www.moomoo.com/news/post/5904455/with-300000-pieces-in-12-hours-cristiano-ronaldo-s-jersey
  - Caveat: clubs typically receive only ~4.5–6.25% of replica shirt sale price under licensing deals — SoccerBible analysis, 2021 — https://www.soccerbible.com/news/2021/09/messi-v-ronaldo-the-real-winners-from-their-record-shirt-sales/
- **Messi PSG shirts: reported 150,000 shirts sold rapidly (widely repeated as "in 7 minutes") after his 2021 signing** — unofficial, media-reported figure; treat with caution — e.g. https://brandclickx.com/messi-jersey-sales-revenue-pssg-inter-miami-2026/
- **Messi's Inter Miami No. 10 was the best-selling MLS jersey of 2023 and 2024 (two consecutive years) and the best-selling soccer jersey in adidas's entire portfolio** — MLS official announcement — MLSSoccer.com, 2023 — https://www.mlssoccer.com/news/messi/lionel-messi-inter-miami-superstar-leads-top-selling-mls-jerseys-in-2023 ; 2024 list via https://www.goal.com/en/lists/lionel-messi-best-selling-mls-jerseys-2024-inter-miami-top-10-olivier-giroud/bltcecff9cbb56ab0bc ; Inter Miami surpassed 100,000 kit sales in 2023 — https://www.cbssports.com/soccer/news/lionel-messi-tops-mls-jersey-sales-two-months-after-joining-inter-miami-sergio-busquets-in-top-15-as-well/
- **Argentina/Messi 2022 World Cup shirts sold out worldwide before the final; the post-victory three-star shirt sold out within minutes of release (26 Dec 2022); adidas reported ~$424m in World Cup "event-related sales" in Q4 2022** — adidas confirmations — CNN Business, 2022 — https://www.cnn.com/2022/12/19/business/adidas-leo-messi-jerseys/index.html ; Washington Post, 2022 — https://www.washingtonpost.com/sports/2022/12/20/argentina-champion-jersey-adidas-messi/
- **Annual club shirt sales (2022/23): Liverpool ~1.8m, Manchester United ~1.75m, Real Madrid ~1.7m, Barcelona ~1.6m units** — PR Marketing (Dr. Peter Rohlmann), the standard industry survey — via Footy Headlines / FourFourTwo, 2023 — https://www.footyheadlines.com/2023/11/best-selling-club-football-kits.html ; https://www.fourfourtwo.com/gallery/ranked-10-clubs-who-sell-most-replica-shirts
- **2025 shirt sales: Real Madrid ~3.13m units, Barcelona ~2.94m** — Statista compilation, 2025 — https://www.statista.com/statistics/1118294/football-shirt-sales-by-club/
- **Kit/merchandising revenue 2023/24: Real Madrid €196m, Bayern Munich €171m, Barcelona €171m, Liverpool €146m, Manchester United €120m** — via OneFootball report summary, 2024 — https://onefootball.com/en/news/top-20-european-clubs-by-revenue-from-replica-shirt-sales-new-report-39133762

**Conflicts and caveats (Sales records).** Shirt-sales figures are the least reliable numbers in football: brands almost never publish unit sales. The Rohlmann/PR Marketing survey (~1.6–1.8m units for the biggest clubs, 2022/23) and the Statista 2025 figure (~3m+ for Real Madrid) cannot both describe the same thing — methodology and what counts as a "shirt" (all replica products vs. jersey units) differ. The Ronaldo £32.5m and Messi-PSG 150k figures come from a retail aggregator and unverified media reports respectively, not the brands. The Nigeria "3 million pre-orders" is an NFF statement that Nike never publicly confirmed. Use all of these with attribution language ("according to…", "reported…"), never as bare fact.

## 4. Custom Teamwear Industry Lead Times

(The one section where supplier sites are the legitimate primary source. All figures as stated by the supplier, retrieved August 2026.)

- **owayo (DE/US, made-to-order sublimation): production currently ~3 weeks, ranging 2–5 weeks depending on time of year; courier delivery an additional 2–3 business days (EU/US); express production available at a 15–50% surcharge depending on urgency** — owayo delivery pages, 2026 — https://www.owayo.com/productservice/delivery-us.htm and https://www.owayo.com/cycling/faq-us.htm
- **Spized (DE, made-to-order sublimation): ~2 weeks from order confirmation to delivery ("sometimes even quicker")** — Spized order-process page, 2026 — https://www.spized.com/us-en/order-process-delivery-time
- **KitKing (UK, branded stock kit + printing): printed personalised kit lead time 2–3 weeks; Express Service guarantees processing and dispatch in 3–5 working days (in-stock product only, excludes courier time)** — KitKing FAQs and Express Service pages, 2026 — https://www.kitking.co.uk/pages/faqs and https://www.kitking.co.uk/pages/express-service
- **Sporty Kits (UK, sublimated): fully sublimated kits delivered in 2–3 weeks** — Sporty Kits, 2026 — https://sportykits.co.uk/
- **Custom Sports Clothing UK (sublimated): production 16–18 days after payment and artwork approval** — Custom Sports Clothing, 2026 — https://www.customsportsclothing.co.uk/
- **O'Neills (UK/IE, bespoke manufacturer) publishes a dedicated lead-time page** — https://teamwear.oneills.com/en/leadtime — exact current figure not captured in this research (page blocked automated retrieval); historically quoted around 4 weeks for bespoke. Verify manually before citing a number.

**Conflicts and caveats (Lead times).** The industry clusters around **2–3 weeks production for sublimated made-to-order kit**, stretching to 4–5 weeks in peak season (owayo explicitly says production time varies by time of year — direct evidence that pre-season demand spikes stretch lead times). "Delivery in X weeks" claims differ in what they include: Spized quotes door-to-door, owayo quotes production only (+2–3 days shipping), KitKing's express covers dispatch only. Retailer-model suppliers (KitKing: branded stock + print) are structurally faster on express but depend on stock availability; manufacturer-model suppliers (owayo, Spized, O'Neills) are consistent but capacity-bound.

## 5. Seasonality of Team Kit Orders

- **England: grassroots and youth seasons run roughly with the school year — fixtures start the first weekend of September and finish April/May; pre-season training and friendlies begin 4–6 weeks earlier (late July/August)** — Diamond Football guide — https://diamondfootball.com/3855/when-is-the-grassroots-football-season-in-the-uk ; Football Parent — https://www.footballparent.co.uk/parent-guides/what-is-grassroots-football
- **Kit-ordering advice in the UK market: orders placed by July land in time for a September season start; mid-August orders "rarely land in time for the first match" given supplier lead times** — Optimum Sport new-season kit guide, 2026 — https://www.optimumsport.com/blogs/featured/new-season-new-kit-your-grassroots-guide-to-getting-ready-for-2026-27
- **A junior team typically needs new match kit every 2–3 seasons; a full 16-player set costs around £630** — Kit Funder grassroots sponsorship guide, 2025/26 — https://kitfunder.ai/blog-posts/grassroots-football-sponsorship-guide
- **Germany: amateur (Kreisliga) seasons kick off early-to-mid August** — e.g. Kreisliga Nord 2026/27 opening fixtures in mid-August — OberpfalzECHO, 2026 — https://oberpfalzecho.de/sport/fussball/kreisliga-nord-die-saison-startet-am-freitag — implying a June–July ordering window, several weeks earlier than the UK.
- **Supplier-side corroboration of the seasonal spike: owayo states production time "varies depending on the time of year" (2–5 weeks)** — owayo, 2026 — https://www.owayo.com/productservice/delivery-us.htm
- **US: youth club soccer runs a two-season calendar (fall season starting August/September; separate spring season)** — creating two annual order spikes (summer and winter) rather than Europe's single dominant summer peak. *Not independently verified in this research pass — season structure is common knowledge in US youth soccer but should be pinned to a US Youth Soccer/league calendar URL before citation.*
- **France/Spain/Italy: amateur competitions likewise start in September** (with licences issued from summer — the FFF's 2.38m licences accumulate from the July/August registration window). *Directionally solid, but no single citable primary URL captured; verify against FFF/RFEF/FIGC district calendars if a precise claim is needed.*

**Conflicts and caveats (Seasonality).** Editorial can safely say: European amateur seasons start August (DE) to September (UK/FR/ES/IT), so custom kit ordering peaks June–August, and mid-August orders miss kickoff at standard 2–5-week lead times. Avoid stating exact "order-spike" percentages — no supplier publishes order-volume seasonality data; the evidence is indirect (lead-time stretching + buyer guides).

## 6. Market Size: Consumer Spending on Football Merchandise

- **Global licensed football (soccer) merchandise market: USD 11.32bn in 2024, projected USD 15.89bn by 2030 (6.0% CAGR)** — Grand View Research, 2024/25 — https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/licensed-football-merchandise-market-report
- **Alternative estimate: USD 11.48bn in 2024, to USD 18.85bn by 2032 (6.4% CAGR)** — Data Bridge Market Research, 2024 — https://www.databridgemarketresearch.com/reports/global-licensed-football-merchandise-market
- **Broader football merchandise market projected to hit USD 24bn by 2032** — Global Market Insights via Yahoo Finance, 2024 — https://www.gminsights.com/industry-analysis/football-merchandise-market
- **All-sports licensed merchandise market: USD ~38.0bn (2025), projected USD 59.4bn by 2033 (5.9% CAGR); apparel is the largest product segment (66.8% revenue share, 2023); offline channels still dominate (67.7%, 2023)** — Market Research Future / Grand View Research, 2024–25 — https://www.marketresearchfuture.com/reports/licensed-sports-merchandise-market-7068 and https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/licensed-sports-merchandise-market-report
- **Top 20 richest clubs' combined revenue: €12.4bn in 2024/25 (+11% YoY), first time over €12bn; commercial revenue (sponsorship + merchandising) €5.3bn = 43% of total, the largest revenue stream for the third straight year** — Deloitte Football Money League, 29th edition, 2026 — https://www.deloitte.com/uk/en/services/consulting-financial/analysis/deloitte-football-money-league.html
- **Real Madrid: ~€1.2bn total revenue 2024/25, including €594m commercial — the commercial line alone would rank top-10 in the Money League** — Deloitte Football Money League 2026 — https://www.deloitte.com/uk/en/about/press-room/real-madrid-tops-deloitte-football-money-league.html
- **adidas: ~$424m in World Cup event-related sales in Q4 2022; football category +30% in the first nine months of 2022** — adidas figures via Washington Post, 2022 — https://www.washingtonpost.com/sports/2022/12/20/argentina-champion-jersey-adidas-messi/

**Conflicts and caveats (Market size).** Market-research firms' figures ($11.3bn vs $11.5bn vs a $24bn trajectory) differ because scope differs (licensed-only vs all football merchandise vs all sports). Cite one firm per article and name it. Deloitte's numbers are the most defensible in the set but measure club revenue, not consumer merchandise spend — don't conflate. None of these isolate *teamwear/custom kit* as a segment; no credible public figure for the custom teamwear market was found.
