# Product architecture — Collections / Studio / Bespoke

Written 30 July 2026. This is the canonical taxonomy for how MOMUTO presents
its offer, agreed while planning the model-page expansion (`docs/10x-plan.md`
lever 5). Every page, PDP, homepage block and piece of copy should be
consistent with this document. When a new surface doesn't know how to describe
something, the answer is here.

## The thesis

Owayo and Spized are kit **suppliers**: configurators whose templates load as
blank shapes in default colours. MOMUTO aspires to be a sports **brand**. The
difference is opinions: a supplier has capabilities, a brand has finished
designs it stands behind — named, curated, dropped, worn. The visible proof is
already in the product: a Spized template loads flat blue; a MOMUTO design
loads finished, in an intentional colourway. This architecture makes that
difference legible to customers and to search.

## The three doors

Every customer is standing in exactly one of three doors, and every page
should make it obvious which one.

### Door 1 — The Collections *(the brand)*

Finished designs by the MOMUTO studio. Scarce on purpose.

- **Ready-to-Play** — the performance line. 7–12 designs, refreshed as drops,
  never more. Curated colour combinations, worn by real teams, preview in 24h.
  **−10%** vs custom.
- **Concept collections** — the collabs and country/national concept series
  (Asperion France, Blue Samurai Japan, regions of Spain, the coming
  France/Italy/Japan series). Unofficial by design, no federation crests.
- **Iconic Series** — the culture line (world-cup moments tees). Lifestyle,
  not teamwear; it exists to say *brand*, not *supplier*.

Defining properties: designed **to completion**; **named** (THE APEX, not
"design 14"); **dropped** (numbered drops, dates); **worn** (real teams in the
gallery). Customisation within a Collection = your crest + our curated
combinations. You are buying our design.

**The −10% story (tell it exactly like this):** you skip the design phase
because our studio already did it and production has already validated it.
You are not paying for design work, so you don't. That is the whole reason,
and it is true.

**Scarcity rule:** RTP does not grow past ~12. New models do NOT default into
RTP — the discount and the curation are one signal, and diluting the line
dilutes the brand. Additions happen as *drops that replace or retire*, the way
a collection works.

### Door 2 — The Studio *(the tool)*

The 3D configurator and its base designs. This is where the model expansion
(7 → 25+) lives.

- Base designs are **starting points, never blank canvases** — every template
  loads looking finished, in an intentional colourway. This is a hard product
  rule and the visible moat vs Owayo/Spized: even our starting points are
  designed.
- **Full price** (from €21.90 at 10+). No discount, because the customer does
  the designing. The tool itself is free.
- Each model gets a **product page per locale**: presented as a design object
  (name, design story, style vocabulary — striped / gradient / retro / camo /
  sash / hooped…), with one primary CTA: **open it in 3D**, deep-linked with
  the model pre-loaded. These pages are the SEO estate (keyword-shaped names
  and descriptions) and the front doors into the tool.
- Model pages never claim to be finished products. Their promise is "finish
  this yours" — which is why they don't compete with or confuse the RTP line.

### Door 3 — Bespoke *(the service)*

Our designers make it for you, from anything: an AI render, a sketch, a
moodboard, a brief. **€15 deposit to put a designer on it, credited in full
from 5 jerseys** — free for a team order. First mockup 24–48h, revisions
included. The refund promise lives on the gate page only.

## The pricing logic, in one breath

> **Bespoke takes a deposit because our designers work. Studio is base price
> because you do the designing. Ready-to-Play is −10% because the design work
> is already done and validated — you skip the step everyone else charges
> for.**

Three doors, three honest price stories. Never explain a price any other way.

## How the doors reference each other

- Collection PDPs may say "want to change more than colours? → open the base
  in the Studio" (RTP models are also loadable in 3D — that stays).
- Studio model pages may point up: "prefer it finished? → Ready-to-Play" and
  down: "want something no template can do? → Bespoke".
- Bespoke pages show the transform examples (concept → production) and may
  point at the Studio as the free self-serve alternative — that framing is
  already live on the request pages.
- The three-path section on the request-design pages IS this architecture and
  should adopt this vocabulary as pages get touched.

## What this unlocks (and what it forbids)

Unlocks:
- The 25-model Studio expansion with zero brand confusion — new models are
  Studio inventory with SEO-shaped identities, not discount-line dilution.
- Basketball slots cleanly in: Studio basketball models first (the tool
  already has the body), an RTP basketball capsule later *as a drop* when the
  studio has designs worth standing behind.
- Country concepts are a Collection — full price, branded, dropped — not
  Studio templates.

Forbids:
- Growing RTP as a dumping ground for every new model.
- Studio templates that load as blank shapes or single flat colours.
- Any page that offers a discount without the "design already done" story.
- Calling Studio templates "ready" or Collections "templates".
