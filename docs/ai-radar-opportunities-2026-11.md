## 5. Opportunities, ranked (each one is a page or a line, not a campaign)
1. **A price page per market** (EN/US/ES/FR/IT): "What a custom kit costs in 2026", the ladder in plain text, a worked total for 10–15 players,
   shorts/socks/long sleeves/fast lane, the deposit line. Spized wins price prompts because its numbers are quotable; ours live in FAQ JSON-LD
   and the jersey-maker hub only. Highest value: price is 7 prompts, 1–13% for us, and the engines answer "it depends" otherwise.
2. **One canonical category page per market, in the engines' words**: www `custom-soccer-jerseys` (and the UK: "custom football kits", Sunday
   league, Royal Mail), es `equipaciones-futbol-personalizadas`, fr `maillot-foot-personnalise`, it (new). Each: facts in the first 100 words
   (no minimum, 25–30 days, from €21.90 at 10+, 92/8 fabric, Europe-based studio), the three sentences the engines quote about the leaders turned
   into ours, FAQPage. These are the URLs the source map says get cited.
3. **Italy**: nothing to cite today. Italian comparison page (`it-site-recovery` first), then the IT category page, then the IT AI hub. Jersix owns
   every Italian prompt with one page.
4. **Use-case pages per market** (friends/12 jerseys, company tournament, Sunday league): Vistaprint wins them by saying "name, number and size
   per player in the configurator". We do that; we do not say it on a page the engines read. ES/FR/IT versions of the EN use-case posts.
5. **"From scratch, not templates" on the request page** (EN/US/ES/FR/IT): the bespoke prompt is lost to companies that use those words.
6. **Third-party roundups**: get listed in `clupik.com/blog/equipaciones-futbol-personalizadas`, `impressionante.es`, `aranzulla.it`, UK Sunday-league
   roundups. The ES ones cite 10+ suppliers and never us. One email each with the facts and the comparison page as source.
7. **Fix what Perplexity re-reads**: find the page still carrying "20–25 days" (likely a cached copy of the old comparison post) and request a
   recrawl; add the 25–30 line to every comparison page's first paragraph.
8. **Basketball maker on www + "Custom Ink alternative" wording on the US basketball page** (T5): the engines' default for basketball is Custom Ink.
9. **Perplexity vs Claude split**: Claude reads our fact pages (35%); Perplexity reads catalogue/category pages and roundups (4%). Items 1–3 are the
   Perplexity fix; the AI-assistants post is the Claude reinforcement.
