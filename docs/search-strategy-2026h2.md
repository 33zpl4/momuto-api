# Search & Growth Strategy — H2 2026

Based on GSC exports (last 6 months, to 2026-07-15) for the domain property
(momuto.com, includes all subdomains) plus the fr. and es. subdomain
properties. Written July 2026, after the big model shift: free design → 15€
deposit, RTP collection revamped (colour toy + "Make it yours in 3D" into the
3D tool), 3D tool substantially improved.

## Where we are (the data)

**Trend: strong and accelerating.** Domain clicks 255/mo (Jan) → 2,441 (Jun),
July running ~2,700/mo pace. Average position improved from ~24 to ~11.
June impressions spiked to 74.9k (≈3× April) — Google is testing us on far
more queries; rankings haven't caught up with the exposure yet. That gap IS
the opportunity.

**The four decisive facts:**

1. **EN's non-brand engine is one commercial cluster sitting on page-1
   bottom.** "custom football jersey / jersey maker / football shirt maker /
   jersey design maker / football kit maker" ≈ **50k impressions at positions
   7–12** (~1,100 clicks). The homepage carries it (5,177 cl / 169,931 impr,
   pos 15.4). Moving pos 7–11 → top 3–5 roughly 3–4×es non-brand EN clicks
   on impressions we ALREADY get.
2. **The US is impressions-rich, rank-poor.** 30,202 US impressions, position
   27.6, just 200 clicks. The "soccer" query cluster (92 queries, ~6.8k impr)
   averages pos 35–40 with ~zero CTR: Google already shows us for "custom
   soccer jersey maker"-type queries, on page 3–4. The constraint is
   rank/relevance ("soccer" terminology, US signals), not exposure.
3. **The concept/AI angle is already our biggest untapped impression pool —
   especially FR.** "maillot concept (france/foot)" ≈ 5,300 impr at pos ~9
   with only ~45 clicks; the concepts collection (4,687 impr) and the
   l'art-des-concepts post (6,181 impr) both sit pos ~7–8. The
   creer-maillot-foot-ia-chatgpt-gemini post is FR's best-clicking blog
   (80 cl, pos 6.4). EN mirror pages rank similarly. Nobody owns
   "AI concept → real kit" yet.
4. **ES's proven-but-unbuilt play is local city pages.** The Madrid page gets
   30 cl / 1,565 impr at pos 19 (barely optimised!); Canarias pos 7,
   Zaragoza pos 13.8 — tiny pages, they still rank. Barcelona, Valencia,
   Sevilla, Málaga, Bilbao etc. don't exist yet. Also: the one competitor
   page (zentral-opiniones) is ES's #2 non-brand asset (22 cl, pos 7.3) —
   comparison pages work in ES like the comparatif does in FR (FR's
   comparatif-fournisseur is the single best non-brand page: 359 cl, pos 7.7).

## The plan, ranked by impact ÷ effort

### P1 — this month

**A. Homepage restructure around the new funnel** *(answers Q4 + Q1)*
The homepage is our biggest asset (170k impr) and still tells the old story.
New structure, top to bottom:
1. Hero = the two self-serve paths: **RTP collection** ("ready to play —
   customise in 3D") and **3D designer** ("design from scratch"). One
   screenshot-quality render each, real prices ("from 16.90€ / −10% RTP").
2. RTP model strip (the toy thumbnails link straight to product pages).
3. "How it works" 3-step (choose/customise in 3D → we check & prep artwork →
   delivered) — keep the professional-designer assurance prominent.
4. **Pro design as the premium tier**: "Want it fully bespoke? Our designers
   build it — 15€ deposit, credited to your order." Deposit framing =
   seriousness filter, say so plainly.
5. Social proof band (teams, quotes — see LLM layer below) + AI-concept
   gallery teaser.
Title/H1 targets the commercial cluster: "Custom Football Kits — 3D Kit
Maker & Ready-to-Play Designs | momuto".

**B. Push the 3D tool site-wide** *(Q1)*
It was underpromoted because it was weak; it isn't any more. Nav item
("3D Kit Designer"), CTA blocks on every blog post and collection page,
and upgrade the two 3D-configurator blog posts (EN+FR, both pos ~8 with
3–4k impr) — refresh content, add live tool screenshots, internal-link hard.

**C. ES city pages** *(Q3)* — template exists and ranks. Ship
**Barcelona, Valencia, Sevilla, Málaga, Bilbao, Zaragoza(refresh),
Madrid(refresh), Murcia, Alicante, Galicia/Vigo** on
`/pages/camisetas-futbol-personalizadas-<ciudad>`:
local league/tournament names, 2–3 RTP models rendered in club-typical
colours (the toy makes this cheap), 3D tool CTA, delivery-to-city note,
FAQ block with FAQPage schema, footer/hub interlinking
(equipaciones hub → cities). Madrid at pos 19 → top-5 is the benchmark win.

### P2 — next 6 weeks

**D. Own "AI concept → real kit"** *(Q5)* — we genuinely have the pipeline
(mockup → momuto 3D model → printed kit) and most new gallery models ARE
AI-origin. Build a hub section (`/pages/ai-concept-to-kit` EN/FR/ES):
- The process, honestly documented (concept in, designer-checked artwork,
  3D preview, production) with real before/afters from the gallery.
- Tag AI-origin models in the gallery; each gets a short "born from a
  concept" story blurb.
- Blog series feeding it: "we turned a ChatGPT/Gemini concept into a real
  kit", "what AI gets wrong about kit design (and how our designers fix
  it)", FR versions first (the impressions are already there), ES after.
- This simultaneously attacks FR's "maillot concept" 5k-impression pool
  (pos 9 → top 5) and the EN "ai football kit" queries (8k impr, ~0 CTR).

**E. LLM-quotability layer** (the "according to…" play — ES already sees
useful LLM referral traffic):
- Named founder + designer quotes on About/process/AI-hub pages.
- Team testimonials with club names and short Q&As on team pages.
- Crisp Q&A blocks (FAQPage schema) on: pricing, minimums, delivery,
  deposit-vs-RTP, AI-concept process. One-sentence quotable answers.
- 1–2 small data posts per quarter ("what 500 fan concepts taught us about
  kit design") — original numbers are what LLMs cite.
- Organization + Person schema; consistent naming so citations resolve.

**F. FR consolidation** *(Q8)*: comparatif page pos 7.7 → top 3 (refresh
2026 data, add momuto-vs-X sections); concept cluster via D; add one more
alternative/vs page (owayo/spized FR variants already prove the pattern).

### P3 — validate then scale

**G. US: content first, subdomain when data says go** *(Q2)*
Phase 1 (now, cheap): make www speak soccer where it counts — beef up
`/pages/design-your-own-soccer-jersey` into a proper US hub (it ranks pos 8
on only 280 impr today), soccer-termed titles on 2–3 blog posts, RTP pages
get "soccer jersey" secondary phrasing, USD price mention + US delivery
time on the hub. Target: soccer cluster from pos ~35 to page 1–2.
Phase 2 (trigger: >300 US non-brand clicks/mo or soccer cluster reaching
page 1): dedicated **us.momuto.com** store — fits the existing multi-store
infra (fr/es pattern), USD pricing, US-sizing, hreflang en-US/en. Not
before: a subdomain won't fix rank by itself and splits authority while
we're still building it.

**H. ES beyond cities** *(Q7)*: 1–2 more "alternativa/opiniones" pages
(the zentral page is repeatable), push the 3D-creator cluster ("creador de
camisetas de fútbol 3d" already pos 6.5 — a dedicated landing beats a blog
post), keep feeding LLM-referral surface via E.

### P4 — parity checklist

**I. Italian site** *(Q6)* — currently ~invisible (12 clicks). Checklist:
deposit model + copy, RTP collection pages with 3D links (needs: IT slugs
in CONFIG3D/STORE3D in embed.js, rtp-content.js `it` locale — deliberately
deferred until now), THEN the checkout dependency: **IT has no
checkSumbit.html DiyFile and GoodInfoAction doesn't route to the IT store**
(see design-momuto/server-patches/README) — both must ship before any IT
RTP→3D→cart flow works. Content parity (about/size-guide/FAQ) last.

## Measurable targets (review ~Oct 1)

- EN commercial cluster ("custom football jersey" etc.): pos 7–12 → **≤5**;
  non-brand www clicks 2×.
- ES: 10 city pages live; Madrid **top 5**; ES non-brand share from 29% →
  **50%**.
- FR: comparatif **top 3**; concept cluster pos ~9 → **≤5**.
- US: soccer cluster page 3–4 → **page 1–2**; then decide us. subdomain.
- AI hub live in EN+FR with ≥4 supporting posts.

## Notes on method

- Domain-property exports include all subdomains; www-only numbers above are
  domain minus fr/es rows. GSC exports cap at 1,000 queries; buckets are
  computed on the capped set (directionally right, absolutely understated).
- June's impression spike (74.9k) with flat CTR is normal "testing" breadth;
  the July position improvement (11.4 avg) suggests it's consolidating.
