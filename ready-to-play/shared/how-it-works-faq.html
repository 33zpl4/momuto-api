Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need before you order.

Do I pay now, or after I see a mockup?

You pay when you order. Within 24 hours we send a final mockup of your exact kit — and we don't print a single piece until you greenlight it. Want a change? Free. Your purchase is protected: you never get a kit you didn't approve.

What fabric are the kits made from?

A lightweight, breathable polyester-elastane blend with stretch for full freedom of movement. Every design is sublimation-printed — the colours and logos are dyed into the fabric, so they won't crack, peel or fade like a printed transfer.

How do I choose the right size?

We carry a full youth-to-adult size range. Use the size guide (the "Find your size" link when you add your team) to match each player by chest and height. Between sizes, or mixing kids and adults? Just ask before you greenlight your mockup.

Jersey only or full kit — what's included?

Your choice. Jersey only is the shirt. Full kit adds matching shorts at a bundle price. Pick on the product above — the price updates instantly by quantity.

Is there a minimum order? How does pricing work?

No minimum — order a single kit or hundreds. The price per unit drops as your order grows (every tier is shown in the estimate above), and the Ready-to-Play range is 10% cheaper because the design is already done. Design is always free, with no hidden fees.

How long until it arrives?

We send your final mockup within 24h of ordering. Once you greenlight it, production and delivery take roughly 25–30 days. Need it for a specific date? Tell us before you order and we'll confirm.

Will it come out exactly like the preview?

Yes — and you'll see it first. After you order, our team vectorises and cleans your crest & sponsor, refines placement, and sends a final mockup within 24h. Nothing goes to print until you greenlight it.

Can I add player names and numbers?

Absolutely. Add a name and number per player in your team list at checkout, in your choice of font and colour — mix and match across the whole squad.

What if something isn't right on the mockup?

Change it — free. You can tweak anything right up until you greenlight your mockup, and we only start production once you've approved. No wrong prints, ever.
