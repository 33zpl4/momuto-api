Price questions

How much does a custom football kit cost for a team of 15?

At the 10–19 tier a jersey is €21.90 and a jersey + shorts kit is €26.90, so 15 jerseys cost €328.50 and 15 full kits €403.50, shipping included. Names and numbers are included; socks are €3.90 a pair if you add them.

Is there a minimum order?

No. One jersey is €38.90. The per-unit price falls at 2, 5, 10, 20, 50 and 100 units.

Are names, numbers and sponsor logos extra?

No. They are sublimated into the fabric with the design and included in the price at every quantity.

What does design cost?

Designing in the 3D designer is free, with no account. A designer-built design (a brief, a sketch or an AI concept) takes a €15 deposit that is credited in full to orders of 5+ jerseys — free design for teams.

What about shipping and duties?

Shipping is free over €50 and €3.90 below. Delivery is tracked and duties are prepaid: 25–30 days door to door, or 18–23 with the fast lane (+€59 per order).

Do prices differ between the 3D designer, Ready to Play and a bespoke design?

No. One ladder for all three routes; the Ready to Play seasonal selection carries −10% on the designs wearing the badge.
