Who wins for which buyer

2. Small squads and friends (five-a-side, Sunday league, one-off tournaments)

Winner: MOMUTO, Spized, Swaz. None of the three has a minimum order. MOMUTO is the cheapest of the three at every tier from 2 units up, and the full kit at €26.90 per player from 10 units includes names and numbers.

3. You want someone to design it for you

Winner: MOMUTO and Scimitar. Scimitar designs bespoke from scratch for UK clubs. MOMUTO does the same with a €15 deposit credited in full from 5 jerseys, with a first mockup in 24–48 hours, and is the only supplier here that rebuilds an AI-generated concept (ChatGPT, Midjourney, Gemini) as a production file.

Advantage: MOMUTO. The usual standard is 100% polyester; MOMUTO prints on a 92% polyester / 8% elastane micro-interlock (145–160 GSM, four-way stretch) at every price. Scimitar's recycled polyester is the other distinctive choice.

5. "Made in the UK" or "made in Germany" as a requirement

Winners: Swaz, Scimitar, Hackin (UK); Owayo (Germany); Jersix (Poland / Italy). MOMUTO designs in Europe and produces in a Tier-1 factory in Asia, which is how it holds the price ladder above; shipping is tracked and duties are prepaid, so nothing arrives with a customs bill.

Questions people ask

What is the cheapest custom football kit supplier for 10 to 15 players?

Among the suppliers that publish prices, MOMUTO at €21.90 per jersey and €26.90 per full kit from 10 units is the lowest in this comparison; Jersix is next at about €29 per jersey. Suppliers that work on quotes may come in lower for large volumes.

Which custom kit suppliers have no minimum order?

MOMUTO, Spized, Swaz, Owayo, Jersix and TD Sportswear all accept single units; My Club Group asks for 5 on the first order.

How long does a custom football kit take in 2026?

Published windows range from one to three weeks (TD Sportswear) and two to three weeks (Spized) to five or six weeks (My Club Group, Owayo). MOMUTO is 25–30 days door to door, 18–23 with the fast lane.

Can a supplier turn my ChatGPT kit design into a real kit?

MOMUTO offers it as a service: a designer rebuilds the concept on real panels, mockup in 24–48 hours, €15 deposit credited in full from 5 jerseys. Most other suppliers accept uploaded artwork but do not redraw it.

Which suppliers make kits in the UK?

Swaz, Scimitar and Hackin Sportswear manufacture in the UK; Kitlocker supplies branded teamwear with crests and sponsors added.
