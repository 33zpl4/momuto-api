How a claim works

Five steps, and most claims never reach step four — a clear photo is usually enough for us to reprint or refund without the parcel coming back.

  • Within 7 daysWrite to us. Email info@momuto.com within 7 days of delivery with your order number, a short description and photos of the issue (and of the parcel if it arrived damaged).
  • 1–2 daysWe review and issue an RMA. A team member checks the photos against your approved mockup and replies with a Return Merchandise Authorization number and next steps. Please don't ship anything back before you have it.
  • If neededSend it back. Some cases need the item at our production facility for inspection. Ship it unused, in its original packaging with tags attached, tracked, quoting the RMA — we refund the postage once the return is approved.
  • On receiptInspection. The returned item is checked against the claim. Exchanges ship only after the inspection is complete; reprints for mockup mismatches start immediately.
  • Within 14 daysRefund or replacement. Approved refunds go to your original payment method within 14 days of approval, for the full amount paid plus any return shipping. Replacements re-enter production and ship tracked.

Questions about returns

Can I exchange a jersey for a different size?
No — custom items are printed with the name, number and size you entered on the roster, so a size swap would mean reprinting at our cost. Use the size guide before ordering; if in doubt, size up. If a garment measures differently from the published chart, that is a defect and we replace it.
Will the colors match what I see on screen?
Closely, but not perfectly. Screens emit light and fabric reflects it, so a slight tone difference is normal on every custom jersey and isn't covered as a defect. We run color tests before production to keep it minimal. For a critical color, give us the Pantone or hex code and we match the print to the reference, not the screen.
Do I need to send the item back?
Often not. For visible print or seam defects, photos are enough and we reprint or refund straight away. We ask for the item back only when an inspection is needed — and then we refund the return postage once the claim is approved.
What if I missed the 7-day window?
Write to us anyway. The window keeps claims tied to delivery, but a genuine defect found on first wear is still a defect. We look at every case.
How long does a replacement take?
A reprint re-enters production (7–12 days) and ships tracked (10–15 days), so plan on 25–30 days door to door — the same timeline as the original order. Tell us if there is a match deadline and we will prioritize it.
Where does my refund go?
To the payment method used at checkout, within 14 days of the claim being approved. If it hasn't appeared after that, check with your bank first — posting times vary — then email info@momuto.com and we will trace it.
Can I return a Ready to Play jersey I customized?
The same rules apply: it was printed to your approved mockup, so returns are for defects, damage or a mismatch with the mockup only.
