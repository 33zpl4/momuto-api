Soccer jersey maker FAQ

Is the soccer jersey maker really free?
Yes. Designing in the 3D tool is free and needs no account. You pay only when you place an order, and the price is the ladder above — no setup, art or screen fees.
Can I make a single soccer jersey?
Yes. There is no minimum order. A single jersey is $45.90; the per-jersey price falls to $25.90 at 10 and $19.90 at 100.
Can I upload my own logo and sponsor?
Yes. Upload PNG, JPG, SVG or PDF artwork in the maker. A designer cleans every crest and sponsor before production and sends you the file for approval.
Does the maker handle names and numbers?
Yes. Add the roster on the cart with each player's name, number and size. Names and numbers are included in the price; long sleeves are +$3 per player.
What if I don't like the result?
You approve a production mockup before anything prints, with unlimited revisions. If a jersey arrives defective or different from the approved mockup, we reprint or refund it — see the returns policy.
Can I use an AI-generated jersey design?
Yes. Send a ChatGPT, Midjourney or Gemini concept through the AI concept page and a designer rebuilds it as a print-ready soccer jersey.
How long does a custom soccer jersey take?
Mockup in 24–48 hours, 7–12 days production, 10–15 days shipping: plan on 25–30 days door to door. The fast lane (+$69) saves about a week.
