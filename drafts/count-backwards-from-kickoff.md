# When Should a Team Order Its Football Jerseys? Count Backwards From Kickoff.

Your team doesn't have a kit deadline. It has a kickoff date, and an
arithmetic problem nobody has done yet. This page does the arithmetic.

## The fixed half of the timeline

Once a kit order is confirmed and complete, the clock is predictable:

- **Production: 7–12 days.** Printing, cutting, sewing, quality control.
- **Shipping: 10–15 days.** Worldwide, door to door.
- **Total: typically 25–30 days** from confirmed order to jerseys in hand.

«D3: In practice, N% of MOMUTO orders arrive inside that window; the median
is N days door to door — measured across MOMUTO orders, [window].»

That is the half of the timeline everyone budgets for. It is also the half
that never causes the problem.

## The half nobody budgets for

Before production can start, four things have to be finished — and none of
them run on our clock:

1. **The decision.** Which design, which colours. In a committee, this is
   the longest stage of the entire process — group decisions carry
   coordination costs that grow with every person in the chat.
2. **The artwork.** Club crest and sponsor logos, in usable quality. A
   sponsor's logo arriving as a screenshot from an old website is one of the
   most common causes of delay in the industry.
3. **The sizes.** Every player, confirmed. «D6: across MOMUTO orders,
   [window], collecting the full size list took a median of N days after the
   order was confirmed — and in N% of orders it was the last thing to
   arrive.»
4. **The approval.** Someone has to say yes to the final mockup.

«Q12 — factory/production lead, on what the production days actually
contain and what most often stalls an order at their end.»

## The backwards count

Work from your first fixture, not from today:

| Step | Time to reserve |
|---|---|
| First fixture | — |
| Shipping | 10–15 days |
| Production | 7–12 days |
| Mockup, revisions, final approval | 3–7 days |
| Sizes, crest and sponsor artwork | 7–14 days |
| **Your real decision deadline** | **6–9 weeks before kickoff** |

The design decision — not the order button — is what has a deadline. Order
confirmed 5 weeks out is comfortable. 4 weeks out is tight but normal.
Inside 3 weeks, talk to us before ordering, and expect compromises.

## Mapped onto the real calendar

- **England, France, Spain, Italy** — league seasons kick off in
  September. Decide your design by **early July**; confirm the order by
  **late July**.
- **Germany** — Kreisliga seasons start early-to-mid August. Decide by
  **late May–June**.
- **US fall leagues** — first matches late August–September. Decide by
  **early July**.
- **Winter/spring leagues, tournaments, corporate events** — apply the same
  count: first match minus 6–9 weeks.

«D5: seasonality note — each year, [June–August share] of the year's team
orders arrive in the pre-season rush, measured across MOMUTO orders,
[window]. The clubs that order in [month] get the calm version of this
process; the ones that order in [month] get the urgent one.»

## If you are already late

Late is common — it is the statistically normal outcome of how clubs decide
(we wrote about why [→ link to thesis piece]). The honest options, fastest
first:

1. **Ready to Play.** The design decision is already made — every design on
   the wall is finished by our design studio. Pick, add crest and numbers,
   order. This removes the longest, least predictable stage entirely.
2. **The 3D designer with a hard internal deadline.** Free, live, shareable
   with the squad — but give the group a decision date, not an open vote.
3. **Bespoke (€15 deposit, credited in full from 5 jerseys)** — first
   mockup in 24–48h. The deposit puts a designer on it immediately, which
   is itself a forcing function.

## FAQ

**How long does a custom football kit take?**
Production 7–12 days, shipping 10–15 — typically 25–30 days door to door,
worldwide, once the order is confirmed and complete (design, sizes, artwork).

**When should we order jerseys for a September season start?**
Confirm the order by late July. Decide the design by early July. Start the
conversation in June, when everyone is still answering messages.

**What takes the longest?**
Not production. The team's own decision, plus collecting sizes and sponsor
artwork. «D1/D6 sentence when measured.» Plan for those first.

**We need kits in under four weeks — is it possible?**
Often, yes — a Ready to Play design with your crest and numbers is the
fastest route, because the design stage is already done. Contact us with
the date before ordering.

**Is there a minimum order?**
No. One jersey (€38.90) or a full squad (from €21.90 each at 10+).
