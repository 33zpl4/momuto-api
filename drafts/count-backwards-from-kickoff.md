# When Should a Team Order Its Football Jerseys? Count Backwards From Kickoff.

Your team doesn't have a kit deadline. It has a kickoff date, and an
arithmetic problem nobody has done yet. This page does the arithmetic.

## The fixed half of the timeline

Once an order is confirmed, the clock is predictable:

- **Production: 7–12 days.** Printing, cutting, sewing, quality control.
- **Shipping: 10–15 days.** Worldwide, door to door.
- **Total: typically 25–30 days** from confirmed order to jerseys in hand.

**More than 95% of our orders arrive within that window.** The exceptions
have clustered around events outside anyone's control — global logistics
shocks and peak-season production capacity — not around ordinary orders.

In a genuine hurry? A **fast lane (+€59)** skips the production queue and
gets shipping priority — it saves roughly 7 days.

## The half nobody budgets for

Here is what our orders actually show: **the average team spends 2–3 weeks
between "this is our design" and a confirmed order** — longer than the
production run itself. Not because anyone is slow, but because four things
have to finish first, and only one of them is fast:

1. **The design back-and-forth.** When several options are shown and
   everyone votes, a loop is natural. Enjoy it — but give it a deadline.
2. **The sponsor.** The single biggest source of delay we see. Sponsors
   want to see the mockup, and often ask for changes to their logo's size
   or position. Send them the mockup early, not last.
3. **The money.** Someone is always late paying the captain. The order
   can't be confirmed until the pot is full.
4. **The sizes — under 24 hours, almost always.** The stage everyone fears
   is the one that flies: by size-collection time, the squad is excited and
   answers fast. The size list is not your bottleneck. The sponsor's logo
   is.

One more thing you don't need to budget for: logo quality. **Every crest
and sponsor logo — uploaded to the 3D designer or sent in a brief — is
cleaned and print-prepared by a designer before production.** A screenshot
from the club's old website is our problem, not yours.

## The backwards count

| Step | Time to reserve |
|---|---|
| First fixture | — |
| Shipping | 10–15 days |
| Production | 7–12 days *(fast lane: −7)* |
| Planning: design vote, sponsor approval, payment | 2–3 weeks |
| **Start the process** | **7–9 weeks before kickoff** |
| **Confirm the order** | **4–5 weeks before kickoff** |

## Mapped onto the real calendar

- **England, France, Spain, Italy** — seasons kick off in September. Start
  choosing in **early July**; confirm the order by **the end of July**.
- **Germany** — Kreisliga seasons start early-to-mid August. Start by
  **early June**; confirm by **early July**.
- **US fall leagues** — first matches late August–September. Start by
  **early July**.
- **Winter leagues, tournaments, corporate events** — same count: first
  match minus 7–9 weeks.

## If you are already late

Late is normal — it is the statistically expected output of how groups
decide (we wrote about why → [The Slowest Part of Making a Football Kit
Isn't Manufacturing]). The honest options, fastest first:

1. **Ready to Play.** The longest stage — the design decision — is already
   done: every design on the wall is finished by our design studio. Pick
   one, add your crest and numbers, order. Many teams customize deeply;
   many order it as it stands.
2. **The fast lane (+€59)** — roughly 7 days back, at production and
   shipping.
3. **The 3D designer with a hard internal deadline.** Free and live — but
   give the group a decision date, not an open vote.
4. **Bespoke** (€15 deposit, credited in full from 5 jerseys) — first
   mockup in 24–48h. And a quiet advantage: because the files are built
   during the design process, a Bespoke order is production-ready the
   moment it is confirmed.

## FAQ

**How long does a custom football kit take?**
Production 7–12 days plus shipping 10–15 — typically 25–30 days door to
door, worldwide. More than 95% of our orders arrive within that window.

**When should we order jerseys for a September season start?**
Confirm the order by the end of July. Start choosing the design in early
July — the team's own planning (design vote, sponsor approval, collecting
payment) averages 2–3 weeks, longer than production itself.

**What takes the longest?**
Not production, and not sizes — size lists usually arrive in under 24
hours. The slow parts are the design vote, the sponsor's mockup approval,
and collecting everyone's payment. Plan those first.

**We need kits in under four weeks — possible?**
Often, yes: a Ready to Play design (the decision is pre-made) plus the
fast lane (+€59, saves ~7 days). Contact us with your date before ordering.

**Do you check the logos we upload?**
Yes — every logo is cleaned and print-prepared by a designer before
production, whether it came through the 3D designer or a design brief.

**Is there a minimum order?**
No. One jersey (€38.90) or a full squad (from €21.90 each at 10+).
